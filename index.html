      Fake Unpaid Toll Texts (Smishing)

      Scammers impersonate E-ZPass, SunPass, FasTrak, TxTag, NTTA, I-PASS, Peach Pass, RiverLink, Good To Go, and ExpressToll using lookalike links ending in .xin, .top, .vip, or .xyz demanding small urgent payments like $6.99.

      Fake DMV Suspension & “Reply Y” Texts

      Messages from foreign country codes (such as +63), random Gmail/iCloud iMessage accounts, or group texts threatening immediate driver's license suspension or telling you to “Reply Y, then reopen” are designed to bypass phone spam filters and steal card details.

      Real vs. Fake Parking & Camera Tickets

      Legitimate US citations include a real citation number, license plate, exact date/location, and a verified .gov or official municipal payment portal. If your ticket checks out as REAL, use CarDesk Ticket Appeal AI before late fees double.

      US TOLL & DMV SCAM FAQ
      Quick answers for US drivers
      Is that unpaid E-ZPass, SunPass, or FasTrak text a scam?

      Almost always yes. Real US toll agencies (E-ZPass, SunPass, FasTrak, TxTag, NTTA, I-PASS, Peach Pass) do not text random links threatening immediate license suspension or asking you to pay via unofficial domains.

      Why does the toll text say “Reply Y, then exit and reopen”?

      That is a well-known iMessage smishing trick. iPhones disable links from unknown senders until you reply once. Replying “Y” activates the phishing link so scammers can lead you to a fake payment page.

      Does the US DMV text about driver's license suspension?

      No. State DMVs never collect overdue fines through random text message links or threaten arrest within 12–24 hours. Always log in directly by typing your state's official .gov website.

      I got a toll text from a +63 number or Gmail/iCloud address — is it fake?

      Yes, 100% fake. Official US toll roads and state DMVs never send violation notices from international numbers (like +63), personal email addresses, or multi-person group chats.

      What if my parking or speed camera notice is actually REAL?

      Check the due date immediately so late fees don't double your fine. If the ticket seems unfair or has an error, use CarDesk Ticket Appeal AI to build a state-specific dispute packet in 60 seconds.

      Is my screenshot stored or shared anywhere?

      No. Screenshots and photos are compressed on your device, analyzed live by AI, and discarded immediately. No account or credit card is ever needed.
